## 5. Regional Moz DA & Backlink Acquisition Protocol

### Objective
Elevate Domain Authority (DA 1 $\rightarrow$ DA 15+) and eliminate link spam vulnerability through clean local citations.

### Execution Checklist
1. **Google Business Profile (GBP) Verification:**
   - Add geo-tagged photos of the Macherla studio.
   - List opening hours (Mon–Sat: 09:00 - 19:00 IST).
   - Set primary link to `https://www.palnadu.dev/` and appointment link to `https://www.palnadu.dev/contact`.
2. **Submit Disavow File:**
   - Upload `docs/seo/disavow.txt` via Google Search Console Disavow Tool to disarm incoming `.shop` PBN links.
3. **Structured Local Entity Linkage:**
   - Every citation must link to either the homepage (`/`) or relevant local landing page (`/locations/macherla`).
   - Use branded and semi-branded anchors (`Palnadu.dev`, `Palnadu Software Studio`, `Palnadu.dev Macherla`).
   - Strictly avoid commercial exact-match anchor stuffing on third-party directories.
